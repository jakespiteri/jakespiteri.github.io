Map data

London elevation contours are derived from Mapzen Terrain Tiles, primarily Copernicus EU-DEM, with SRTM, GMTED2010 and ETOPO1 in some tiles. Elevation samples were lightly smoothed before contouring. The map is decorative and is not suitable for navigation or surveying.

Europe terrain data produced using Copernicus data and information funded by the European Union — EU-DEM layers. SRTM and GMTED2010 courtesy of the U.S. Geological Survey. Modifications are not endorsed by USGS. ETOPO1: DOC/NOAA/NESDIS/NCEI — National Centers for Environmental Information. U.S. Government source material is not subject to copyright protection in the United States.

Land, park and water colours are derived from an Esri World Topographic Map export. The source colours were isolated and recoloured; roads and labels were omitted. This is not authoritative land-cover data.

Basemap sources: Esri, HERE, Garmin, Intermap, increment P Corp., GEBCO, USGS, FAO, NPS, NRCAN, GeoBase, IGN, Kadaster NL, Ordnance Survey, Esri Japan, METI, Esri China (Hong Kong), © OpenStreetMap contributors, and the GIS User Community.
